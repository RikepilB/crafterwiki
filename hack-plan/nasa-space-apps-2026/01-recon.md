# Recon: NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026

**14–15 November 2026 · hybrid (in-person and virtual Local Events + the virtual Universal Event) ·
theme “The Next Frontier!”** · run 2026-09-14 · corpus slug `nasa-space-apps-2026`, prior
`nasa-space-apps-2025`.

## Intake assumptions (defaults — replace when the team is known)

| Question | Assumed |
|---|---|
| Team | 4 people, may grow to 6 (the maximum): two web/full-stack developers, one data or science person, one design/story person |
| Pitch language | English for the project page (required); Spanish or English for a live Local Event pitch |
| Local Event | An in-person LATAM Local Event if one is near; otherwise the Universal Event |
| Hardware | Laptops only |
| Hours | The Local Event's schedule within 14–15 Nov (up to 48 h) |
| Required tech | At least one NASA data source or resource (expected, as in 2025) |
| Ideas already in mind | None — and none may be worked on before the event (see rules) |

## Rules that change the harness

- **Teams must not begin working on the challenges before the hackathon** (organizer FAQ). Challenge
  summaries appear 2026-09-17 and full statements 2026-10-28. Until 14 November this plan can only
  produce recon, patterns, *angle shapes* and general preparation — no challenge-specific solution,
  design or code.
- Teams of at most six, all registered for the same Local Event (or all in the Universal Event).
- Submit through the team page on spaceappschallenge.org by the end of the Local Event (23:59 local
  time on the last day in 2025).

## Rubric and lens weights

| Criterion (stated) | Lens | Weight | Stated / inferred |
|---|---|---|---|
| Impact | `impact` | 20% | Stated for 2025; carried to 2026 until the Judging & Awards guide appears (2026-11-13) |
| Creativity | `originality` | 20% | Same |
| Validity | `scientific-validity` | 20% | Same |
| Relevance | `challenge-relevance` | 20% | Same |
| Presentation | `storytelling-pitch` | 20% | Same |

Each criterion is scored 1–5 at every judging level. Equal weights are an assumption: no percentages
are published. The organizer's own advice (2024 guide) is that teams do well on the first four criteria
and under-invest in presentation.

## Judging funnel

| Phase | Who | Output | Team present? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Local Event or Universal Event judges | Global Nominees (about 1 in 10 projects in the 2024 guide) | Depends on the Local Event format |
| 2 | NASA and Space Agency Partner subject-matter experts | 45 Global Finalists + Honorable Mentions (2025) | No — they review the submission *(inference)* |
| 3 | Executive committee of agency leaders | 10 Global Awards | No *(inference)* |

**Implication:** two of the three phases most likely judge the submission without the team in the
room. The project page, demonstration link and project link are the pitch for the global phases; the
live pitch matters only locally.

## Prizes and lanes (2025 award definitions)

| Award | Definition | Lens | 2025 winner shape | Fit for the default team |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Best Use of Science | Best and most valid use of science / scientific method | validity | Molecular dashboard on one GeneLab dataset (`spacegenes-plus`) | Medium — needs a science lead |
| Best Use of Data | Makes space data accessible or leverages it uniquely | design, originality | Exoplanet pipeline with false-positive checks (`resonant-exoplanets`) | High |
| Best Use of Technology | Most innovative use of technology | technical depth | Personal weather-risk app (`skysense`) | High |
| Galactic Impact | Most potential to improve life on Earth or in the universe | impact | Debris compliance platform (`astro-sweepers`) | Medium |
| Best Mission Concept | Most plausible concept and design | validity, relevance | Habitat survival simulator (`pureflow`) | Medium |
| Most Inspirational | Captures our hearts | storytelling, impact | National satellite broadband concept (`photonics-odyssey`) | Medium |
| Best Use of Storytelling | Communicates open data through storytelling | storytelling | Illustrated space-weather story (`hercode-space`) | Medium — needs illustration |
| Global Connection | Connects people around the world through technology | impact | Orbital data-center optimizer (`gaia-leo`) | Low |
| Art & Technology | Combines technical and creative skills | design, technical depth | Farming game on NASA imagery (`zumorroda-x`) | Medium |
| Local Impact | Greatest potential for local impact | impact | Andean reforestation for water security (`quenaris`) | **High** for a LATAM team |

**Stacking:** in 2025 each award went to a different team, so plan for one award per team
*(inference)*. Local Awards are separate and do not qualify a team for global judging.

## Constraints and submission requirements (2025 rules unless noted)

- Project page in English; a demo video in another language needs English subtitles.
- Use and link at least one NASA data source or resource.
- Disclose every AI tool used; watermark AI-generated images and video; acknowledge AI-generated code,
  data, audio and text.
- No NASA branding (logo, flag, mission identifiers).
- “Create Your Own Challenge” projects are not eligible for global judging.
- 2026: no work on challenges before the event; team size ≤ 6; submission guide published 2026-11-13.

## Saturation and past editions

- **No base rates.** The corpus has the Global Winners only (`roster_coverage: winners-only`): 10 of
  11,500+ submissions in 2025 (18 challenges) and 10 of 9,996 in 2024 (20 eligible challenges). Nothing
  here says which lanes are crowded.
- *Commercializing LEO* produced 3 of the 2025 winners and the agriculture challenge 2 of the 2024 winners —
  a challenge can yield several awards; that does not mean it was uncrowded.
- LATAM teams won 3 of 10 awards in both editions (2025: Mexico, Brazil, Peru; 2024: all Brazil).

## Domain data availability

NASA resources the 2025 winners named on their team pages: POWER API, Earthdata (Giovanni, GES DISC,
earthaccess), DONKI space-weather database, GeneLab (OSD-288), MAST archive (Kepler, TESS, JWST, Hubble),
Landsat and MODIS vegetation indices (NDVI, EVI, NDMI), FIRMS fire data, LAADS DAAC, the U.S. Drought
Monitor, Orbital Debris Program Office tools (Debris Assessment Software, ORDEM), and the NASA Image and
Video Library. Account setup and learning these APIs is general preparation, not challenge work.

LATAM public-sector data (via Croma) is only relevant if a 2026 challenge touches public services; not
applicable yet.
