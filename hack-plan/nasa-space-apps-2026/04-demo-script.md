# Demo script: local decision with a live validity check

Template for the merged angle. Bracketed parts are filled on 14 November, once the challenge is chosen.

## One sentence

“For **[person]** in **[place]**, we turn NASA **[dataset]** into **[one decision]** — and show when not to
trust it.”

Repeatability test: a judge should be able to say it to another judge without the words API, model or
dashboard.

## Local Event pitch (default 3:00; use the Local Event's format)

| Time | Beat | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Hook: the person and what is at stake this week | Photo or map of the place |
| 0:15–0:35 | Problem in one sentence, with one number that matters there | The number |
| 0:35–1:45 | **Moment:** a judge's city and date → the decision, the dataset and its date, the confidence | Live app |
| 1:45–2:15 | Depth: the validity check — the same query where the data disagrees or is stale, and what the app does | One visual |
| 2:15–2:45 | Impact: who uses it first and how it reaches them | Short list |
| 2:45–3:00 | Close: the sentence again, and the ask | Title card |

## Real vs mocked

| Part | Status | Disclosure |
|---|---|---|
| NASA data query for the demo region | Real | Dataset name and date on screen |
| Decision logic | Real, deterministic | Method in one line on the page |
| Confidence / validity check | Real | Shown, not hidden |
| Other regions | Slide only | Say “next” |
| Any AI-generated text or image | Disclosed | Listed in the AI section; images watermarked |

## Fallback

- Cached data snapshot for the demo region, switched on by the demo driver if the network or the feed fails.
- Recorded screen capture of the moment, labelled as a recording if used.
- A second laptop with the build open; a second person who can present.

## Stage plan

- Speaker: story/pitch lead. Driver: app lead. Data lead answers validity questions.
- Nothing on the table: this event does not reward props (0 of 20 hardware winners in 2024–2025).

## Global judging variant (the submission wins without you)

Mirror the section order every 2025 winner used on its team page:

1. **Summary** — first line is the one sentence; then person, place, decision, and why the data can be trusted.
2. **Project Demonstration** — link to a short video that opens on the decision (length and format per
   the 2026 Project Submission Guide, published 2026-11-13; do not assume).
3. **Project** — live site or repository that still works after the event.
4. **Project Details** — how it works, the validity check, tools, what is next.
5. **Use of Artificial Intelligence** — tool by tool, what each did and what the team built (the 2025
   Best Use of Technology winner is a good model).
6. **NASA Data** — every dataset, named and linked.
7. **Space Agency Partner & Other Data** — everything else, named and linked.

Checklist: English throughout (subtitles for other languages) · at least one NASA resource linked · AI
disclosure and watermarks · no NASA branding · all members registered at the same Local Event.

## Rehearsal checklist

- Two full timed runs before submission; cut anything that runs long.
- One run with the network off to test the fallback.
- A teammate who did not build it reads the page cold and says the sentence back.
